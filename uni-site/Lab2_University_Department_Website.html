About Our Department

The Department of Computer Science at ABC University provides education in computing and information technology.

Our goal is to help students understand computer science concepts and apply them to real-world problems.

Our Mission

To provide quality education, encourage research and develop responsible technology professionals.

Academic Programs

BS Computer Science

Four-year undergraduate program covering programming, databases, networks and software development.

Advanced study in computing, research methods and specialized areas of computer science.
